## ★ Featured Projects

### [Realm Explorer](https://github.com/frostjade71/RealmExplorer)

A Minecraft Server & Realm discovery platform that helps players find their next adventure. Browse, search, filter, and shuffle through community servers and realms with ease.

`JavaScript` · `Vue.js` · `Discord OAuth` · `REST APIs`

- Browse and filter servers by gamemode — SMP, Skyblock, Factions and more.
- Vote for your favorite Realms and Servers of the Month with live leaderboards.
- Server owners can list and manage their servers through a dedicated dashboard.

### [FacultyLink](https://github.com/frostjade71/FacultyLink)

A web-based Teacher Tracking and Profiling System designed to streamline campus monitoring and academic visibility, with role-based dashboards and an integrated AI assistant.

`PHP` · `MySQL` · `Docker` · `Leaflet Maps`

- Privacy-first design — students see teacher status only; GPS location is admin-exclusive.
- Built-in "LinkyBot" AI chatbot to help students find teachers and check schedules instantly.
- Interactive Leaflet maps for administrators to monitor faculty locations in real-time.
